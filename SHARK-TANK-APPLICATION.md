## 5. The problem

A farmer loses money in three places, and almost never finds out.

**He sells at the wrong mandi.** On a single day we checked live government
market data, wheat sold for ₹2,000 per quintal in Raisen and ₹2,826 in
Ratlam. Same crop, same day, same state — a gap of ₹826 per quintal. On ten
quintals that is ₹8,000 a farmer never sees. Not because of a bad harvest,
but because nobody told him.

**He reads a crop problem too late.** By the time damage is obvious to the
eye, the field is usually past saving. Expert advice exists, but it is a bus
ride and a working day away.

**He misses the insurance window.** Under the national crop insurance scheme,
a localised loss must be reported within 72 hours. Miss it and the claim can
be rejected outright, regardless of how real the loss was.

Existing apps solve exactly one of these. A farmer standing in a struggling
field still has to open three different apps, or make three phone calls, or
give up.

## 6. The solution

BhoomiX puts all three answers on one screen, in the farmer's own language.

It is not a prototype or a mock-up. It is a working product with fifteen live
pages, running today, that anyone can open in a browser without installing
anything.

## 7. What is actually built

**Crop Intelligence** — Photograph a leaf. The app identifies the crop and its
growth stage, names the likely problem, gives a confidence score, and
suggests a remedy the farmer can actually buy. Includes a reference library
of common crop problems with symptoms and immediate actions.

**Mandi Prices** — Daily rates pulled from the Government of India's open data
platform, covering thousands of regulated markets. Filter by crop and by
state, see the modal price plus the full low-to-high band. This is the
feature that closes the ₹826 gap.

**Crop Loss Reports** — Photographs are stamped with GPS coordinates and the
exact time of capture, paired with recorded rainfall for those coordinates,
inside a visible 72-hour countdown. Exports one printable evidence pack
formatted for the insurer.

**Government Schemes** — Ten central and state schemes explained in plain
language: who can apply, what you actually get, and a direct link to the
official application page. Most farmers miss schemes because nobody explains
them simply.

**Crop Advisory** — An assistant that answers farming questions in the
farmer's language, by text or by photo, at any hour.

**Agri Market** — Seeds, fertiliser, tools and equipment, linked directly from
the diagnosis that recommended them.

**AgriNova Mart** — Everyday groceries and household supplies with a
10-15 minute delivery promise, because a farming household buys more than
seed.

**Nearby Shops and Orders** — Find local agri dealers on a map, place an
order, and track it to the door.

**Farming Guides** — Organic, vegetable and modern farm technology, explained
without a sales pitch.

**Built for who actually farms** — 23 Indian languages, not just English and
Hindi. Sign-in by mobile number for farmers with no email address at all.
Separate views for farmers, delivery partners and administrators.

## 8. What makes us different

**We answer "what do I do next," not just "what is wrong."**

Other crop apps in India are good at identifying a problem. Open one and
check: no mandi rate, no insurance help, no way to buy the remedy it just
recommended. They are built for the whole country rather than for one
district's reality.

|                        | Other crop apps | BhoomiX |
|------------------------|-----------------|---------|
| Problem identification | Yes             | Yes     |
| Live mandi prices      | No              | **Yes** |
| Insurance claim help   | No              | **Yes** |
| Buy the remedy         | No              | **Yes** |
| Indian languages       | Some            | **23**  |

**Our real moat: the outcome loop.**

Every crop app in India has millions of photographs of unhealthy plants. Not
one knows what actually fixed them.

Seven days after a diagnosis, BhoomiX asks the farmer one question: did it
work? Cured, better, no change, or worse. One tap. Every answer makes the
next farmer's advice sharper.

That is a dataset that compounds and cannot be copied by launching a similar
app. A competitor with more funding can clone our screens in a month. They
cannot clone three seasons of outcomes. You earn that one harvest at a time.

## 9. Business model

BhoomiX is free for the farmer. That is deliberate — a farmer who is unsure
whether he can afford advice will not ask for it, and we need him to ask.

Revenue comes from the commerce the advice creates:

1. **Commission on marketplace orders** — inputs through Agri Market, essentials
   through AgriNova Mart.
2. **Partner and dealer subscriptions** — local agri shops and nurseries pay to
   be listed, stocked and routed orders through the partner platform.
3. **Delivery margin** — on fulfilled orders through our registered partner
   network.
4. **Future: aggregated, anonymised crop-outcome insight** for input companies
   and insurers — only with farmer consent, and never personal data.

## Before you submit — read this

**Three things that will help you.**

1. **Refresh the wheat numbers.** Mandi rates change daily. Open the app,
   check today's spread, and use the current figure. The point survives
   whatever the numbers are — the gap is always there.

2. **Have the app open on your phone.** Your single biggest advantage is that
   this is real. A Shark who opens it and sees live government mandi data
   loading is worth more than any slide.

3. **Know your unit economics.** The first hard question will be "how do you
   make money and what does it cost you to get one farmer?" Have a number,
   even a rough one, and say how you got it.

**One thing to be careful about.**

The app currently displays a "Trusted by 25,000+ farmers" line in its
interface. That is placeholder design copy, not a real figure. Either remove
it before anyone sees the app, or make it accurate. A Shark who finds an
unverifiable number on your home screen will doubt every other number you
gave them, and that is a very hard hole to climb out of.
