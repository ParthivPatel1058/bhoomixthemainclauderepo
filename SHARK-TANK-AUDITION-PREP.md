## 2. FSSAI — read this properly

Checklist item 27 is **FSSAI licence**.

AgriNova Mart sources fresh produce from farms and delivers it to customers' homes.
Under the Food Safety and Standards Act that makes BhoomiX a **Food Business
Operator**. An FBO needs registration regardless of size.

- **Basic Registration** — for turnover under ₹12 lakh/year. Roughly ₹100 per year.
- Apply at **foscos.fssai.gov.in**
- Often issued within days for Basic Registration.

**Why this matters beyond paperwork:** Indian Sharks ask food businesses about FSSAI
as a matter of routine. If you are asked on camera and the answer is no, it reads as
not having done the basics — which undercuts everything you say about quality and
vetting. If the certificate has not arrived by the 9th, carry the **filed application
receipt**. Applied-for is a completely different answer from never-thought-about-it.

While you are there, check whether you also need **Shop and Establishment
Registration** (item 29) for your state.

## 4. THE PRODUCT PROBLEM — you are software, they expect hardware

The email asks for photo, dimensions, weight, power source and safety declarations.
Those questions assume a physical product. **BhoomiX is a platform.**

Do not leave them blank and do not force a fake answer. State plainly that it is a
digital product, then redirect to what they actually need in order to plan the demo.
Draft in §6.

**The real risk is the internet.** Your product is a live web app. Convention-hotel
wifi is unreliable and you will be one of many applicants on it.

**Mitigations — all three, not one:**

1. **Mobile hotspot** on a charged phone, data confirmed. Test it that morning.
2. **A recorded screen-capture demo video** on the pen drive *and* stored locally on
   the laptop. If the network dies you keep talking over the video and nobody notices.
3. **Screenshots as a PDF** on the laptop, as the last fallback.

A founder whose demo dies with no backup looks unprepared. A founder who says "no
problem, I recorded it" looks like someone who ships.

## 8. LIKELY QUESTIONS — prepare real answers

They will probe the weakest points. These are the ones to expect:

**"Do you have FSSAI?"** — See §2. Have a real answer.

**"What is your revenue?"** — Say the actual number, even if it is zero or tiny.
Inflating it is the fastest way to lose credibility, and the documents you upload will
contradict you.

**"You have no warehouse — how do you guarantee quality?"** — Your best question.
Answer: we go to the farm and the dealer's shop ourselves and check before anyone is
listed. Then give a specific example of a dealer you rejected.

**"Blinkit and Zepto have crores in funding. What stops them?"** — Do not claim they
cannot. Say they *will not* — their model needs dark-store density a town like Pawai
cannot support, which is exactly why nobody serves it.

**"How much have you invested and what do you want?"** — ₹40,000, self-funded,
trademark secured, platform live. Know your ask and your equity number **before** you
walk in. Decide it this week with your brother, and both say the same number.

**"Who does what between you two?"** — Have a crisp split. "We do everything together"
is a weak answer.

**"Is it registered?"** — Whatever the truth is, say it directly, and say what the next
step is.
