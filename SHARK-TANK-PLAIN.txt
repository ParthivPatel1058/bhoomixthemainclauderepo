5. THE PROBLEM
===================================================================

A farmer loses money in three places, and almost never finds out.

He sells at the wrong mandi. On a single day we checked live government
market data, wheat sold for Rs. 2000 per quintal in Raisen and Rs. 2826 in
Ratlam. Same crop, same day, same state. A gap of Rs. 826 per quintal. On ten
quintals that is Rs. 8000 a farmer never sees. Not because of a bad harvest,
but because nobody told him.

He reads a crop problem too late. By the time damage is obvious to the eye,
the field is usually past saving. Expert advice exists, but it is a bus ride
and a working day away.

He misses the insurance window. Under the national crop insurance scheme, a
localised loss must be reported within 72 hours. Miss it and the claim can be
rejected outright, no matter how real the loss was.

Existing apps solve exactly one of these. A farmer standing in a struggling
field still has to open three different apps, or make three phone calls, or
give up.


===================================================================
6. THE SOLUTION
===================================================================

BhoomiX puts all three answers on one screen, in the language the farmer
actually speaks.

It is not a prototype or a mock up. It is a working product with fifteen live
pages, running today, that anyone can open in a browser without installing
anything.

7. WHAT IS ACTUALLY BUILT
===================================================================

CROP INTELLIGENCE
Photograph a leaf. The app identifies the crop and its growth stage, names
the likely problem, gives a confidence score, and suggests a remedy the
farmer can actually buy. Includes a reference library of common crop problems
with symptoms and immediate actions.

MANDI PRICES
Daily rates pulled from the Government of India open data platform, covering
thousands of regulated markets. Filter by crop and by state, see the modal
price plus the full low to high band. This is the feature that closes the
Rs. 826 gap.

CROP LOSS REPORTS
Photographs are stamped with GPS coordinates and the exact time of capture,
paired with recorded rainfall for those coordinates, inside a visible 72 hour
countdown. Exports one printable evidence pack formatted for the insurer.

GOVERNMENT SCHEMES
Ten central and state schemes explained in plain language: who can apply,
what you actually get, and a direct link to the official application page.
Most farmers miss schemes because nobody explains them simply.

CROP ADVISORY
An assistant that answers farming questions in the farmer language, by text
or by photo, at any hour.

AGRI MARKET
Seeds, fertiliser, tools and equipment, linked directly from the diagnosis
that recommended them.

AGRINOVA MART
Everyday groceries and household supplies with a 10 to 15 minute delivery
promise, because a farming household buys more than seed.

NEARBY SHOPS AND ORDERS
Find local agri dealers on a map, place an order, and track it to the door.

FARMING GUIDES
Organic, vegetable and modern farm technology, explained without a sales
pitch.

BUILT FOR WHO ACTUALLY FARMS
23 Indian languages, not just English and Hindi. Sign in by mobile number for
farmers with no email address at all. Separate views for farmers, delivery
partners and administrators.

OUR REAL MOAT: THE OUTCOME LOOP

Every crop app in India has millions of photographs of unhealthy plants. Not
one knows what actually fixed them.

Seven days after a diagnosis, BhoomiX asks the farmer one question: did it
work? Cured, better, no change, or worse. One tap. Every answer makes the
next farmer advice sharper.

That is a dataset that compounds and cannot be copied by launching a similar
app. A competitor with more funding can clone our screens in a month. They
cannot clone three seasons of outcomes. You earn that one harvest at a time.

9. BUSINESS MODEL
===================================================================

BhoomiX is free for the farmer. That is deliberate. A farmer who is unsure
whether he can afford advice will not ask for it, and we need him to ask.

Revenue comes from the commerce the advice creates:

1. Commission on marketplace orders - inputs through Agri Market, essentials
   through AgriNova Mart.
2. Partner and dealer subscriptions - local agri shops and nurseries pay to
   be listed, stocked and routed orders through the partner platform.
3. Delivery margin on fulfilled orders through our registered partner
   network.
4. Future: aggregated and anonymised crop outcome insight for input companies
   and insurers. Only with farmer consent, and never personal data.

BEFORE YOU SUBMIT
===================================================================

THREE THINGS THAT WILL HELP YOU

1. Refresh the wheat numbers. Mandi rates change daily. Open the app, check
   today spread, and use the current figure. The point survives whatever the
   numbers are. The gap is always there.

2. Have the app open on your phone. Your single biggest advantage is that
   this is real. A Shark who opens it and sees live government mandi data
   loading is worth more than any slide.

3. Know your unit economics. The first hard question will be how do you make
   money and what does it cost you to get one farmer. Have a number, even a
   rough one, and say how you got it.

ONE THING TO BE CAREFUL ABOUT

The app currently displays a Trusted by 25000+ farmers line in its interface.
That is placeholder design copy, not a real figure. Either remove it before
anyone sees the app, or make it accurate. A Shark who finds an unverifiable
number on your home screen will doubt every other number you gave them, and
that is a very hard hole to climb out of.
