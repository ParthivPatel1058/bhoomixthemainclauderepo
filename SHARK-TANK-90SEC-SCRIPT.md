# BhoomiX — 90-Second Audition Pitch
### Shark Tank India Season 6 · In-person audition, 9 Sep 2026, The Leela Ambience, Delhi

**Speakers:** Parthiv Patel (SDPS, Indore) · Prayansh (Gear International, Bangalore)
**Language:** Hindi-majority, English only where the term is genuinely English
**No ask, no equity, no revenue figure** — this version is purely *the farmer's
problem and what BhoomiX gives him*, as requested.

> BhoomiX mein har sawaal ka jawaab, ek hi app mein hai.

>

> pata ho.

**[BOTH — ~5 sec]**

> Hum hain Parthiv aur Prayansh. Aur yeh hai **BhoomiX.**

## Timing — read this before you rehearse

Script body is **262 spoken words** (counted, not estimated). What that means:

| Your pace | Runs to |
|---|---|
| Fast, confident (3.4 words/sec) | **77 sec** ✅ |
| Normal (3.0 words/sec) | **87 sec** ✅ |
| Slow / nervous (2.6 words/sec) | **101 sec** ❌ over |

So this script **only fits if you keep the pace up.** Auditions make people slow
down, not speed up — so assume the slow row is what will actually happen on the
day unless you have rehearsed against a stopwatch. If your timed run is anywhere
above 90 seconds, cut in this order — each is one clean line, so removing it
leaves no hole:

1. `Sarkari yojnayein` line (−7 sec)
2. `Mausam` line (−5 sec) — folds naturally into Q&A instead
3. Shorten the close to just *"Wahi connection hai BhoomiX."*

Do **not** cut crop disease detection, mandi bhaav, the 72-hour claim, or
AgriNova Mart. Those four are the ones a panel remembers.

## Delivery notes

- **Time yourself out loud, with a stopwatch.** Reading speed and speaking speed
  are not the same, and Hindi-English code-switching slows most people down more
  than they expect.
- The rapid-fire middle section works because it is *fast*. Do not pause between
  features — the momentum is the point. Pause only before "Aur AgriNova Mart."
- Hit these three words hard: **72 ghante**, **taaza**, **connection**.
- The handoff points are marked. A visible switch between two founders reads as a
  real partnership.
- **Bring the phone with the app open.** If they invite a demo, shoot the
  crop-disease camera flow live. A working demo settles "is this real" faster
  than any sentence in this script.

## One thing to be ready for

You asked for no ask and no numbers in the pitch, and that is a legitimate choice
for a 90-second audition — the brief calls this a pitch, not a negotiation.

But the panel will still ask "kitna chahiye, kitne percent pe" and "revenue kitna
hai" in the Q&A right after. Have both answers ready in your head before the 9th,
even though neither is spoken in the script. If revenue is ₹0, say ₹0 plainly —
two students who put in their own ₹40,000 and bought the trademark is a strong
answer, not a weak one.
