Parthiv Patel is a co-founder of BhoomiX, an agricultural marketplace and advisory platform built for Indian farmers.

About BhoomiX

BhoomiX brings the tools a farmer actually needs into one place: live mandi prices, crop disease identification, government scheme information, and a marketplace for seeds, tools and farm supplies — available in 23 Indian languages.

The platform also runs a delivery partner network, so orders placed by farmers reach them through riders coordinated on BhoomiX itself.
