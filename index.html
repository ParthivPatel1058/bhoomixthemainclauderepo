BhoomiX

BhoomiX is an agricultural marketplace and advisory platform for Indian farmers. Check live mandi prices, diagnose crop disease, browse government schemes, and buy seeds, tools and farm supplies.

  • Agri Market - buy and sell farm produce
  • Kisan Mart - seeds, tools and farm supplies
  • Mandi Prices - live market rates across India
  • Crop Disease - identify and treat crop problems
  • Government Schemes for farmers
  • Kisan Help - farming advisory and support
  • Organic Farming guidance
  • Vegetable Farming guidance
  • Robotic Farming and modern equipment
  • Shop Locator - find nearby agri shops
